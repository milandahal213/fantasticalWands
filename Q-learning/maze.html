Q-learning maze

An interactive walkthrough of how a Q-table learns — and why it breaks when the goal moves.

The setup

The agent starts at S and wants to reach the goal (flag). It has no map and no instructions — only a reward signal: +10 for reaching the goal, −1 for bumping a wall, and a small −0.1 for every ordinary step. Every cell holds four numbers — one per direction (up, down, left, right) — called Q-values. They start at zero and get updated after every move using the Bellman equation:

Q(s,a) ← Q(s,a) + α × [ reward + γ × max(Q(s′)) − Q(s,a) ]

Enter how many episodes to train and click "Train" — entering exactly 1 plays the full step-by-step animation with the tank actually moving square by square; entering more runs training instantly in the background. Learning rate, discount, exploration, speed, and the reward values all live in the panel on the left.

Try this

1. Train for a while, then walk the path — the agent should glide straight to the goal.
2. Click a different open cell to relocate the goal, then walk again without retraining — watch it confidently walk toward the old goal and get stuck.
3. Retrain — the numbers reorganize around the new goal within a couple hundred episodes.
4. Click the house icon and drop the agent anywhere in the maze, then walk it — since every square already has its own Q-values, a trained agent can usually navigate from any starting square without retraining, unlike a hardcoded path that only works from one fixed start.
